Privacy policy

Count Me In. Last updated 21 October 2026.

Count Me In is a wall of free local events. This page explains what information the site keeps and why.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you sign in with Google or with an emailed link. We use it only to recognise you when you come back. We do not receive your Google password.
  • What you post: events you add, including the name, place, date, description and the host name you choose to show.
  • What you click: whether you marked an event as interested or going, and any events you reported.

What other people can see

Events you post, and the host name you typed on them, are public. Your email address is not shown to other visitors. Other people see only totals, such as how many people are going, and not who they are.

How we use it

Only to run the site: to show events, count responses, stop spam, and let you remove your own posts. We do not sell your information, send marketing emails, or show adverts.

Where it is stored

Data is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and the site is hosted by Vercel. Both act as processors on our behalf.

Your choices

  • You can delete any event you posted from the wall at any time.
  • You can sign out whenever you like.
  • To have your account and all of your data deleted, email us at the address below.

Cookies and storage

The site stores a sign-in token and your filter choices in your browser so you stay signed in and the wall remembers your settings. It does not use advertising or tracking cookies.
